“ Our story

Founded in 2010, built on a promise to think beyond school.

Chona and Learning Center was established in 2010 in Okpoma, in the Onicha Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, by proprietor Onuchukwu Godwin. It began as a small nursery and primary school with a straightforward mission: to give children in the community access to disciplined, high-quality education without leaving home to find it.

Over the years, the school grew level by level — from nursery classes into a full primary school, and eventually into a combined institution offering junior and senior secondary education. Today, under the leadership of Principal Amaechi, Vice Principal Goodness Uzoma and Administrator Izuchukwu, Chona and Learning Center serves boys and girls from Nursery 1 through SS3, guided by a teaching staff committed to academic excellence, technological skill and character.

What has not changed since 2010 is the philosophy behind the name: learning here is never meant to stop at the school gate. It is meant to travel with every child, wherever they go next.

Admissions

How to apply

Admission runs on a rolling basis each term. Here is exactly what happens once you reach out to us.

  1. Contact the admission office

    Call or WhatsApp +234 706 762 8608 to confirm the class is open and collect the application form.

  2. Submit your details

    Provide the child's date of birth and full name, parent or guardian's name, a passport photograph, and — for Primary 1, JS1 and SS1 — the last school attended.

  3. Pay the admission fee

    A one-time admission fee of ₦2,000 secures the child's place once the form is submitted.

  4. Resumption

    Applications close in the second week of the term, so early contact is encouraged.
